THE LEGEND

Deep in a forgotten jungle lies a temple no map remembers. Inside, on an altar of black stone, rests the Golden Relic — a treasure older than kingdoms.

You are a fearless treasure hunter. You found it. You took it.

The ground shook. Stone cracked. An ancient Guardian tore itself free from the temple walls, its eyes burning red…

Now you must RUN — through the Whispering Jungle, the Ancient Temple, the Abandoned City and the Underground Tunnels. Grab every coin, dodge every trap, and never stop.

How long can you survive the Infinite Chase?

HOW TO PLAY

← →Swipe left / right to change lanes
↰ ↱Swipe left / right at a corner to turn — miss it and you crash!
↑Swipe up to jump over logs, walls and broken floor
↓Swipe down to slide under beams and fire traps
Magnet pulls in nearby coins
Relic Shield saves you from one deadly hit
Double Gold — every coin counts twice
!Bumping a wall makes you stumble. Stumble twice quickly and the Guardian catches you!

Keyboard: Arrow keys / WASD, Space = jump, P = pause
